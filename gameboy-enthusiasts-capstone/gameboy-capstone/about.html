About Game Boy Enthusiasts

Game Boy Enthusiasts is a community site for collectors, players, and anyone curious about Nintendo’s handheld line—from the original 1989 DMG-01 through the Game Boy Micro.

Our mission

We document hardware variants, share preservation tips, and give newcomers a friendly place to learn. The database highlights common models and notable special editions so you can identify what you own or what you still want to find.

What you’ll find here

  • A browsable database of Game Boy family hardware with year, model code, and short description
  • Community-focused features for collectors and players
  • A simple contact form for questions, feedback, or hellos

Educational use

Product photographs on this site are used for educational and community purposes. See individual page comments for asset provenance and license notes. We aim for clear, descriptive alternative text so images remain meaningful when visuals cannot be seen.
